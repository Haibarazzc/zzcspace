# 樱花古境

## Product
A standalone web experience in zzcspace.com. The user confirmed a personal theme while retaining the reference's Chinese architecture and its interactive exploration. Nine imagined buildings connect to the existing personal introduction, photography, learning, music, interests and journal content. The model is an artistic courtyard, not a historically surveyed site.

## Visual direction
The supplied screenshot is the visual authority: an elevated courtyard diorama, large Chinese serif copy on the left, spare museum-like controls, selectable buildings, three camera presets, orbit, reset and time-of-day selection. Name: 樱花古境. Author: 曾子丞. Keep the blog's other pages unchanged.

Sakura theme requested by the user: abundant pink and ivory cherry-blossom trees, petals falling through the real 3D scene and scattered on stone paths and ponds. Plum and lavender atmosphere, blush typography, softened jade and champagne roofs with warm window light. Dawn is the default. Real geometry, curved roof profiles, batched tiles and lattice details; gradual camera easing, directional shadows, restrained bloom and drifting notched cherry petals. Desktop gives the world most of the viewport; mobile keeps controls reachable and text brief. Reduced motion disables automatic camera and petal animation. Sound is optional and starts only after a gesture.

The user's subsequent cherry-tree photograph overrides the original canopy treatment: dark, crooked trunks, spreading limbs, fine twigs, individual pale flowers and visible gaps. No solid pink canopy volumes. The courtyard sits within continuous valley terrain with surrounding mountain ridges, a stream and an approach path; atmosphere separates the distant scenery from the architecture. The entire surrounding woodland is cherry blossom, forming a sakura valley along the slopes, stream and path. Instanced branching trees with separate flowers replace every evergreen; three geometry detail levels preserve the grove's silhouette while keeping the scene practical to render.

## Acceptance

A horizon-to-zenith sky gradient, cooler fill, warm windows, restrained bloom and nearby branch shadows establish the lighting. Confirm the celestial bodies remain visible above the ridges at desktop and mobile overview sizes.
